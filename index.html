Unclasp

Turn the rings. Free them all.

Rings hold on to their neighbours with clasps. Turn a ring until its gap sits under a clasp and the clasp lets go; a ring with nothing left on it slips off the board. Every level is checked before you see it: it can always be cleared by turning rings alone, without a single tool.

Common questions

A ring won't turn.

A ring that still holds another ring with one of its own clasps is anchored. Free the rings it holds first: turn them until their gaps reach its clasps. A closed ring, without a gap, never turns. If a ring wears a cog, the ring with the cog of the same colour turns with it, and the pair is held while either of them holds something.

What are the hearts for?

From level 4 on, grabbing a ring that is held costs one of three hearts. Lose all three and the level is over; you can try again at once, as often as you like.

How do I get three stars?

Three stars are a perfect clear: no grab at a held ring, no wasted move on a level with a move limit, no hint and no tool. One slip leaves two stars, more leave one. Cleared levels can be played again from the level list to improve them. Stars open new skins.

What are the gold-star levels?

Target levels: free the gold rings within a number of moves. A ring costs one move the first time you turn it and nothing after that, so stopping short and coming back is never punished. Some rings are decoys.

There are new pieces on the board. What do they do?

New kinds of piece arrive every few levels: geared rings, padlocks with a key ring, double clasps, countdown rings, firecrackers, glass rings that turn once, spring rings, magnets and more. Each one comes with a line of help the first time, and the little pictures beside the level number, in the game and in the level list, show which kinds a level has.

How do hints work?

The light bulb points at a ring to turn next. You get three free hints a day; after that, a hint costs coins, or plays a short ad first if you prefer, and only when you ask for it. A hint costs the level its third star.

What are the scissors, the hammer and the second wind?

Tools you can use on any level. Scissors take one clasp off, the hammer knocks one ring off the board, and a second wind lets a failed level go on. You start with a few and the daily puzzle gives scissors. With none left, the button offers one for coins or for watching an ad. No level needs a tool.

What are coins for?

Clearing a level for the first time pays coins: some for the clear, some for each star, and one for every ring that carried a coin. A button on the result card doubles them for a short ad, every fifth level opens a chest, and the daily puzzle pays once a day. Coins buy hints and tools. They cannot be bought with money.

How does the daily puzzle work?

One larger puzzle each day, built from the kinds of piece you have already met. Solving it on the day extends your run of days.

How do I get more skins?

Each skin opens when your total stars reach its price. You can also open one early by watching an ad: tap a locked skin. Skins change how the game looks, never how it plays.

When do I see ads?

Now and then a full-screen ad when you leave a cleared level: never in your first levels, at most every other level and a few minutes apart. There is no banner on the board. Ads that give you something (double coins, a hint, a tool, a skin) play only when you tap for them.

Leaderboards don't open.

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. Sign in under Settings › Game Center on your device, then tap the podium on the home screen again. There are three: total stars, levels cleared and the longest run of daily puzzles.

Will I lose my progress?

Your level, stars, tools and skins are stored on your iPhone only. There is no account to sign in to. They stay through updates, and are deleted if you delete the app.
