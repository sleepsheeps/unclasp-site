Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026 · Applies to Unclasp: Rotate Rings Puzzle for iPhone

Unclasp has no account and no server of ours. Your progress and settings stay on your device. The game is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's ad software collects some data to show and measure those ads, as described below.

Your progress

On your device only

The level you have reached, your stars, the tools you hold, your run of daily puzzles, the skins you have opened and your settings are stored inside the app on your iPhone. We never receive any of it, and it is not copied to iCloud by the app. If you delete Unclasp, it is deleted with it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

What Google collects

Unclasp shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To show ads, limit how often they repeat, measure them and prevent fraud, the SDK may collect:

  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device identifiers: the identifier for vendors and, only if you allow tracking, the advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • advertising data, such as which ads were shown and whether you tapped them;
  • product interactions, such as app launches and ad video views;
  • diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data (launch time, hangs).

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Tracking is your choice

After your first cleared level, iOS asks whether Unclasp may track you across apps and websites of other companies. If you allow it, Google may use the advertising identifier to show ads based on your interests. If you don't, ads still appear but are not personalized with that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Europe, the UK and Switzerland

In these regions, Google's consent form asks first whether ads may be personalized and how your data may be used. No ad is requested before you answer. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Ad privacy choices.

Rewarded ads

Some ads give you something in the game: a hint beyond the day's three free ones, scissors or a hammer when you have none left, a second wind after a failed level, or a skin opened before you have the stars for it. They play only when you tap for them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, Unclasp sends your total stars, the number of levels you have cleared and your longest run of daily puzzles to Apple's leaderboards, where other players can see them with your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we receive only the scores. Unclasp never asks you to sign in when it starts, only when you open Leaderboards.

Purchases and ratings

Unclasp sells nothing: there is no in-app purchase and no subscription. The app may show Apple's standard rating prompt; iOS decides whether it appears.

Permissions

Unclasp asks for one permission: tracking, described above. It sends no notifications, and it does not use your camera, photos, microphone, location services, contacts or health data.

Children

Unclasp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. We collect no personal information ourselves at all.
